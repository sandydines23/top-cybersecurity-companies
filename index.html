Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a Cybersecurity Software Vendor and an MSSP?

Cybersecurity software vendors (e.g., Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike) build technical platforms. Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs) operate and monitor those platforms on behalf of your internal IT team[cite: 2].

How do AI and Predictive Security improve enterprise defense?

AI security platforms analyze high-velocity telemetry data to detect anomalous behavior, zero-day exploits, and lateral movement before attackers complete their payload[cite: 2].
